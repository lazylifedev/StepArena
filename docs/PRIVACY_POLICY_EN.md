# StepArena Privacy Policy (Draft)

Last updated: July 29, 2026 / version 1

StepArena stores steps, distance, walking time, estimated calories, speed, optional height/weight/stride settings, local game progress, and diagnostic state on the device. It has no account, cloud sync, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK and does not transmit these data to an external server.

Health Connect is optional and off by default. StepArena reads Steps only to fill gaps and does not write Health Connect data. Data are not used for advertising, credit, employment, insurance, or sale to third parties.

An export is written only when the user selects a destination. Users can delete StepArena data in Data Management or by uninstalling. Source Health Connect data and permissions remain managed by Health Connect. The app is not designed solely for children. A public support contact and hosted policy URL will be configured before publication.

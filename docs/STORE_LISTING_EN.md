## Full description

StepArena is a local step-tracking game. It records activity with the device step sensor and requires no account or server. Compete using eligible daily steps against NPCs generated on your device, and progress through ranks, weekly leagues, seasons, and achievements.

Review hourly and daily records, walking sessions, distance, walking time, and estimated calories. Optional Health Connect support is off by default and reads Steps only to fill likely tracking gaps.

Data stay on your device unless you explicitly export a ZIP. You can reset game progress or delete all StepArena data. StepArena is not a medical device; measurements may vary. Prioritize safety and avoid excessive activity. Opponents are not real users or AI.

Keywords: step counter, walking, pedometer game, local fitness game
